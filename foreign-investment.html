Foreign Investment and International Trade

Foreign Investment

At CATT, we strive to guide foreign investors around the pitfalls of doing business in Nigeria, and are uniquely positioned to obtain the various statutory permits required to set up a new business or invest in a going concern. The firm has assisted many clients in making a smooth, cost-effective transition into the Nigerian market.

CATT will advise on the best choice of vehicle to enter the Nigerian market, and undertakes start-up advisory, company formation and company secretarial services. We also advise clients on shareholder agreements and other collaboration arrangements such as agency, partnerships and technical service agreements.

Energy, Power, Mining and Oil & Gas

Our oil and gas team offers a wide resource base, local expertise and a long-standing presence in the Nigerian oil industry. We have an in-depth understanding of the sector, its issues and its ever-changing trends. Our experience in this area includes negotiation and documentation of drilling contracts, joint operation agreements (JOAs), and agreements for the sale and purchase of petroleum products, amongst others. CATT legal practitioners have also assisted in structuring and negotiating settlement agreements in host community related disputes.

From time to time, we have been called upon to bring our expertise to bear in respect of complex power-related agreements. We also deal with regulatory issues such as licensing required from the Department of Petroleum Resources.

Shipping

CATT advises on all aspects of carriage by sea, including issues arising from Letters of Credit, Charter parties and bills of lading, with expertise across all areas of maritime law.

CATT is regularly involved with non-contentious work in shipping finance/security documentation and local registration of vessels. Our litigation strength enables us to deal quickly with the arrest and release of vessels. We have represented a broad range of clients, including owners, charterers, cargo interests, underwriters, banks and P&I clubs, and our team prides itself on providing cost-effective, tailor-made solutions to meet our clients' specific requirements.
