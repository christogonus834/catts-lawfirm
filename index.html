Introduction

CONTINENTAL ATTORNEYS (CATT) is a Registered partnership law firm. The firm is located in the commercial centres of three Africa cities: Abuja, Abeokuta and South Africa. We boast an impressive client list spanning both the public and private sectors and ranging from medium/large domestic companies to international corporations.

With over a decade of experience, we draw on our rich heritage of excellence to create a dynamic and successful business firm which sets the highest professional standards. Our client's businesses remain at the core of our practice and our goal is to achieve a good understanding of their business environment thereby ensuring innovative and commercially viable solutions to issues.

CATT appreciates the urgency inherent in every commercial transaction and therefore ensures that our practitioners are immediately accessible to our clients. We always strive to minimize the turnaround time of every transaction, and maintain our competitive edge by dealing with our client's legal needs promptly, decisively and efficiently.

Our Fees

CATT always aims to encourage long-term relationships with its clients and to provide value for money. Our fee structure takes into consideration:

  • The complexity of the matter
  • The seniority of counsel with conduct of the matter
  • The specialized knowledge involved
  • The volume and importance of documents prepared or studied, and the value of the transaction

We also offer hourly billing for foreign clients who are more familiar with this method of charging. CATT is pleased to discuss all arrangements with clients as and when appropriate, and to provide an estimate of legal fees or a breakdown of services rendered upon request.
